Frequently Asked Questions

How do payments work?

Work is typically split into milestones (usually 50% upfront to initiate development and 50% upon final review and satisfaction before going live). Safe and secure payment options are supported.

What if I need custom functionality not listed in a plan?

Every project is unique! If your requirements do not neatly fit into one of the packages, feel free to reach out directly on WhatsApp or Email for a tailored quote based on your exact specifications.

Do you help with domain, hosting, or Shopify setup?

Yes, full guidance and setup assistance for domain registration, DNS records, hosting servers, or Shopify/WordPress accounts is included with every plan.

What technologies do you work with?

I specialize in Shopify (Liquid, Storefront API, custom themes), WordPress (Elementor, WooCommerce, custom themes/plugins), Next.js/React, Tailwind CSS, and Laravel PHP.
